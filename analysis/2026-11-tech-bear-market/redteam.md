# Red-team review (Gemini 3.6 Flash, 6 Nov 2026)

Gemini was given the same facts and the first draft of the plan, and asked to
argue against it. Its verdict: **AGREE WITH CHANGES**.

## Objections it raised

1. **Profile mismatch.** A 39% chance of loss and a −28% 5th percentile don't
   fit a low risk tolerance when 100% of the money goes into high-beta tech.
2. **Fighting the Fed.** The first purchase on 9–10 Nov comes ahead of big
   macro risks: a September hike, a 4.85% 10-year yield, 3.4% CPI, $95 oil
   and 65–83% odds of a 9 Dec hike. Only 4 of 8 bottom signals are on.
3. **Concentration in AI spending.** The investor already holds AMD and
   Nebius. Alphabet's Q3 free cash flow was negative.

It suggested: deploy only 50%; delay the first purchase until after the
Nebius and Nvidia results; drop the rule that buys early on further falls;
move weight from Alphabet to TSMC.

## What changed

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| Deploy only 50% | Partly adopted: a $200 reserve is spent only after a much deeper fall (SOX near 7,200 or the basket −20%) |
| Delay purchase 1 | Partly adopted: purchase 1 cut from $350 to $250 and moved to 10–11 Nov, after the jobs report and TSMC's October sales |
| Drop the buy-early rule | Kept, but only while no pause rule has triggered |
| Reduce Alphabet | Balance-sheet scores cut (GOOGL 5→4, META 4→3); TSMC rose to $225; Alphabet keeps the most because it is the least volatile |

Effect in the Monte Carlo: the chance of losing more than 20% fell from 11.7%
(all at once) to 7.2%, and the 5th percentile rose from $714 to $767, at a
cost of about $27 of average value.
